Let Mai ask you the questions
Explore

Complete step 1 first.

Before trying to fix the prompt yourself, let Mai surface the gaps. Start a fresh Mai conversation, paste your raw prompt from step 1, then add this follow-up:

ask me questions to help refine this prompt further
ⓘ
What to watch for: Mai may ask about your audience, how you'll use the output, what level of detail you need, or how recent "recent" actually means. Each question points to a real gap — some you may not have spotted yourself.

3
Refine it yourself
Refine

Complete step 2 first.

Now that you know what's missing, apply those fixes yourself. Use Mai's questions from step 2 as your guide. A good place to start: restrict the sources to credible ones. Try this in a new Mai conversation:

can we improve this further by restricting sources to reputable news outlets, international development reports, and credible local or regional sources?

Workshop example

can we improve this by restricting sources to reputable news outlets, international development reports, and credible local or regional sources?

Don't feel locked in to this wording. Address whichever gap felt most important from step 2 — a missing time frame, an unclear audience, no output format. The goal is to fix one specific thing deliberately and see if it helps.

ⓘ
Other things worth trying: define your time frame ("in the last 6 months"), name your audience ("for a senior leadership briefing"), specify a format ("as a bullet-point summary with 3–5 key findings"), or clarify what your topic actually covers.
